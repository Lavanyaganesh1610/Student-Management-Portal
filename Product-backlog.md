# Product Backlog — Student Management Portal

## Project Objective
To develop a student-friendly web portal that provides information about the institution, courses, student registration, and contact details.

## Product Backlog

| ID | User Story | Priority | Story Points |
|---|---|---|---:|
| US01 | As a visitor, I want a Home page to view portal information. | High | 3 |
| US02 | As a visitor, I want an About page to learn about the institution. | Medium | 2 |
| US03 | As a student, I want a Registration page to enter my details. | High | 5 |
| US04 | As a visitor, I want a Courses page to view available courses. | High | 3 |
| US05 | As a visitor, I want a Contact page to find contact information. | Medium | 2 |
